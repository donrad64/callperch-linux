Privacy policy

CallPerch · Policy version 1.2 · Effective October 5, 2026 · App 1.2.3

SupportPrivacy

CallPerch is created by Arash Manafirad (KR4GOJ). It searches public amateur-radio licensing records published by the United States Federal Communications Commission (FCC).

Data on your device

Your search queries, local FCC database, watchlist, and preferences remain on your device. CallPerch has no developer-operated accounts, analytics, advertising, or telemetry. Watchlist reminders are local operating-system notifications.

FCC data and connections

Sync connects directly to FCC download servers. As with other web requests, these connections expose network information such as your IP address to the service provider. Research uses the downloaded database and does not send your queries to the developer.

Public FCC records may contain names and mailing addresses. Full street/PO box addresses are hidden by default and shown only when you choose Show public FCC mailing address. Exact-address matching runs locally; the app does not establish who lives with someone. CallPerch does not independently collect or verify these addresses. Shared mailing addresses do not verify shared residence. Contact the FCC to correct licensing records.

Callsign assignment history

The local FCC database also stores public FCC Registration Numbers (FRNs), applicant types, previous callsigns, and raw amateur application relationship fields. Repeated callsign switching summaries link individual license records by FRN and derive a dated assignment timeline locally. These summaries and your inspection choices are not sent to the developer.

A summary identifies a pattern in the available records only. It does not establish a person’s intentions, improper conduct, a rule violation, former-holder eligibility, or a right to a callsign. Missing records, changed FRNs, and snapshot timing can affect the result. A return before an ordinary release estimate is historical context, not an FCC eligibility determination. Verify relevant records directly with the FCC.

External websites

FCC login and other external links open your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies. Direct-distribution versions include an optional Buy Me a Coffee link; the App Store candidate omits it. Payment information is handled by that website and is never received by CallPerch.

Retention and deletion

The app sends no search, watchlist, or preference data to the developer, and the developer retains no server-side copy of that data. Downloaded data stays on your device until you replace or delete it. Use Delete local data inside the app to remove downloaded databases, watchlists, preferences, and scheduled reminders. The bundled public snapshot is part of the app and remains until you uninstall it.

For manual removal on macOS, quit CallPerch and delete its container under ~/Library/Containers/<bundle identifier>/ (the current development identifier is app.callperch.mac) and its CallPerch folder in Application Support. The direct version uses ~/Library/Application Support/CallPerch/; the sandboxed version keeps Application Support inside its container. Remove remaining CallPerch preferences and notification permissions through macOS settings if needed. On Linux, quit CallPerch and delete $XDG_DATA_HOME/callperch/, or ~/.local/share/callperch/ when XDG_DATA_HOME is unset; disable the optional user reminder timer if installed. Uninstalling alone may leave local data behind.

If you email support, your email provider and the developer’s provider process that voluntary correspondence. It is used to respond to your request and retained only as needed to resolve it; you may request deletion by contacting either address below.
